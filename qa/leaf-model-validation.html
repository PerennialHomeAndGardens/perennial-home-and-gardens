Leaf photo comparison: measured limits

Perennial Home & Gardens · October 22, 2026

Experimental comparison, not a diagnosis. This model can be confidently wrong on real photographs. Use the symptom checklist and seek Extension confirmation for important decisions. It does not identify arbitrary species, insects, roots or every disease.

What we actually tested

Greenathon MobileNet leaf model, 38 classes, tested in Chrome against 46 deterministic PlantDoc test images. The crop was supplied from the dataset label; this is not a species-identification test. Every selected case is included, whether right or wrong.

  • First suggestion matched the dataset label: 20 of 46 (43.5%).
  • Expected label appeared in the first three suggestions: 34 of 46 (73.9%).
  • Suggestions meeting the display threshold: 18; correct among those: 12.

The display threshold is a raw model score of at least 70%, with a 15-point lead over the next label for the selected crop. These are model scores, not calibrated diagnostic probabilities. Below this threshold, the app says no reliable photo suggestion. Even above it, field errors remain possible.

Supported crops

Apple, cherry, corn, grape, peach, bell pepper, potato, strawberry and tomato. Coverage differs by crop: some have only one disease class plus a healthy reference. Conditions outside the 38 training labels can be misclassified as a known class. A healthy label never establishes that a plant is healthy.

Privacy and loading

The runtime and model are served from this website only when requested. The initial download is about 15 MB. Inference runs locally on the CPU; no photo is posted to a model provider. Photos are temporary. Saved reports contain model suggestions and observations, not image files.

Limits of this check

PlantDoc supplies labeled internet/field images. Labels have not been independently re-adjudicated by a plant pathologist. This small sample is not a guarantee of future performance. Physical iPhone/Android cameras, operating-system print dialogs and real printers are not available in this test environment.
