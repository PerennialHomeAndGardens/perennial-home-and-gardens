How we chose and ordered the collection

This is Perennial’s editorial collection, not a bestseller list or an objective historical ranking. We weigh practical usefulness, influence, distinctive ideas and relevance to home gardeners. Older works are read in their historical context; local extension guidance and current safety advice take priority.

Jimmy Williams and Susan Heeger’s From Seed to Skillet appears at #12. Jimmy is Wayne’s uncle. We disclose that personal connection and also value the book’s link between family history, growing food and cooking it.

Each guide identifies a particular edition, gives our original summary and selection rationale, and links to bibliographic sources. Covers come from Open Library or publishers, with credits on each guide.
