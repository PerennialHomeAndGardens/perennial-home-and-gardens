Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Perennial Home & Gardens LLC may collect, use and protect information submitted through this website.

Information we may collect

  • Name, email address and information you submit through contact, signup or free-resource forms.
  • Information you voluntarily provide about your garden, interests, service needs or project.
  • Basic browser or device information if analytics or security tools are enabled.

How information may be used

  • To respond to questions and service requests.
  • To provide requested resources and website updates.
  • To improve tools, educational content and website performance.
  • To protect the website from misuse or security threats.

Sharing

Perennial does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the website, deliver requested services, process communications or meet legal obligations.

Retention and choices

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, business records, security or legal requirements. You may request correction or deletion by contacting Perennial through the website.

Children

Educational content may be suitable for families, but this website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate adult involvement.

This is a general website policy baseline and should be reviewed as the final website tools, analytics, email platform and payment systems are selected.
