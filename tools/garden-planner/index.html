How this planner works

1. Set your real garden size. The canvas uses feet as its coordinate system, so a 4×8 raised bed is actually 4×8 on the plan.

2. Add objects. Beds, plants, trees, structures, water, paths, compost, seating and sun zones appear in the middle. Drag them where they belong.

3. Teach the map about light. Add Full Sun, Part Sun or Shade zones from the Sun category. The time slider also projects approximate moving shadows from trees, fences, sheds and greenhouses.

4. Select plants for guidance. The Garden Coach shows mature spread, spacing, preferred light, water needs, and companion/avoid relationships. Nearby plants are checked automatically.

5. Save layouts. Plans are stored on this device for now and can be exported as JSON for backup.

Accuracy note: Companion relationships are practical guidance, not guarantees. Sun/shadow is an educational estimate. For precise solar exposure, observe the site through the season or use a professional solar survey.
