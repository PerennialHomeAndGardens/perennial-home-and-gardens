Plant Companions

Search a vegetable, herb or flower to see practical planting relationships—plants that generally fit well together, plants that are usually neutral neighbors, and combinations that deserve extra spacing or caution.

Free to useNo sign-in requiredSave favorites on this device
Companion planting note: Many companion-planting claims come from gardening tradition rather than strong experimental evidence. This tool emphasizes practical compatibility, shared growing conditions, spacing, disease considerations and well-established conflicts where possible. It should guide planning—not promise pest control or higher yields.
