Buy a little extra.Natural materials settle and measurements are rarely perfect. A modest overage helps prevent a second trip.
Measure finished depth.For soil, mulch and compost, use the depth you actually want after spreading—not the height of the bag or pile.
Plant spacing is a starting point.Mature plant width, airflow, light and the variety you grow can change the ideal spacing.
