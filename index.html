Learn with Perennial

Courses that move from “I think” to “I know.”

Three learning levels give Perennial a clear curriculum path—from accessible first steps to more advanced garden systems and leadership-minded planning.

BEGINNER

Gardening Foundations

For gardeners of all ages

Simple, visual instruction built to lower the barrier to entry and make basic gardening concepts feel clear, doable and welcoming.

  • What plants need to grow well
  • Sun, water, airflow and basic care routines
  • Soil basics and starter container growing
  • How to sow seeds and plant transplants
  • Early observation, journaling and habit building
  • Simple harvest basics and garden confidence
INTERMEDIATE

Garden Planning & Production

For gardeners ready to plan with purpose

This level moves beyond basic care into layout decisions, sequencing, seasonal organization and practical production planning.

  • Bed planning, spacing and crop placement
  • Companion planting and plant grouping logic
  • Soil-building routines and amendment timing
  • Seasonal calendars, succession and rotation
  • Pest pressure, plant stress and response habits
  • Harvest timing, record-keeping and improvement
ADVANCED

Advanced Garden Systems

For serious growers and leaders

A deeper level focused on season extension, efficient systems, protected growing and the kind of planning used by committed growers.

  • Protected growing with greenhouse and tunnel thinking
  • Advanced production planning and workflow design
  • Irrigation, inputs and labor-saving systems
  • Integrated pest and disease response planning
  • Documentation, evaluation and seasonal adjustment
  • Leading bigger projects with more structure

About Perennial

A garden can be beautiful and productive at the same time.

Perennial Home & Gardens combines practical growing help, planning tools, education, digital products and local garden support. Food, flowers, herbs, habitat and outdoor living all belong in the same conversation.

Digital resources are designed for gardeners across U.S. growing zones; local services focus on the Washington metropolitan area and Southern Maryland.
