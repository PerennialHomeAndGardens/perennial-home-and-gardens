Emergency Preparation

First-Hour Outage Checklist

For a power outage, including one that also affects water or phone service. Use the steps in the order your situation requires.

Download one-page PDF

If there is a fire or immediate danger, get to safety and seek emergency help. Follow evacuation orders promptly.

First, check people and hazards

  • Check who needs help. Account for your household. If someone relies on powered medical equipment, use their approved backup plan. If critical support is unavailable, seek help and arrange a safe move to a powered location.
  • Stay clear of electrical hazards. Keep away from fallen wires and anything they touch. Stay out of floodwater and leave wet electrical equipment alone.
  • Use battery lighting. Get flashlights or lanterns. Keep generators outdoors, at least 20 feet from doors, windows, and vents. Use a carbon monoxide alarm with battery power or backup.

Next, protect your supplies and stay informed

  • Keep the fridge and freezer shut. Write down when the power went out. An unopened fridge keeps food cold for about four hours. Never taste food to check whether it is safe.
  • Check tap-water notices. Use sealed bottled water if the utility says the supply is unsafe or its safety is uncertain. Follow the exact notice from your water utility or health department.
  • Check for official updates. Use a battery radio or check local alerts, utility updates, and shelter information when you have a signal. Text your household contact where you are and when you will check in again.

Before the hour ends, decide how long you can stay

  • Check what will run out first. Review safe water, medication, medical-device power, and indoor temperature. Relocate before a critical need can no longer be met. Follow evacuation orders promptly.
  • Write down your next move. Choose when to check again and where you will go if you need to leave. Keep your phone, keys, and essential supplies accessible.

Notes to keep handy

  • Time power went out
  • Next check-in time
  • Where we’ll go if we leave
