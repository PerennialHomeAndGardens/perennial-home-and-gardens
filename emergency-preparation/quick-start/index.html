Start with the guidance for what’s happening where you are.

Get the printable first-hour outage checklist

  • Find your situationGo straight to the first steps for a power outage, unsafe tap water, flooding, and other emergencies.
  • Decide whether you can stay safelyUse the long-outage guide when power, water, or other services fail together.
  • Find official alerts and sheltersCheck weather warnings, local notices, and available shelters.

I need a household plan

  1. Fill in your Household Emergency PlanChoose meeting places, record key contacts, and plan for pets, medical needs, and backup destinations.
  2. Download the fillable one-page planSave it offline or print a copy your household can find without internet access.
  3. Check your starting suppliesSee what you have and what you still need to pick up.

I’m stocking food and water

  1. Start with the seven-day no-cook menuScale the shopping list to your household and choose meals from food you already use.
  2. Build a 30-day pantry and water planEstimate what to stock and track what to rotate first.
  3. Write down your water refill planRecord a primary source, a backup, transport, and a refill date.

I’m planning backup power

  1. Enter your battery and devicesEstimate runtime and compare combined running and startup watts with the ratings you enter.
  2. Estimate solar rechargeCompare typical and lower-sun days with your device schedule.
  3. Compare full use with essentials onlySee which devices you could pause and compare both plans over three days.
