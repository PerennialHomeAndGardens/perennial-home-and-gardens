Pantry

Safe water

Water estimates use the CDC baseline of at least one gallon per person per day. Plan extra for pets, heat, illness, pregnancy, and other needs. Meal servings are a planning count. Build meals around the portions, nutrition, and dietary needs of your household.

Decide how you’ll refill

Identify a primary source and a backup. Confirm water is safe and the location is open before traveling. Arrange a ride or a way to carry full containers.

Set the date before your stored supply will run out. If you can’t confirm a safe refill source, use the stay-or-leave guidance.

Build the reserve in stages

First 3 days

Start with drinking water, a manual can opener, and familiar no-cook food. Pair ready-to-eat beans, fish, or another protein with crackers or shelf-stable sides. Include food your household members and pets can safely eat.

Build to 2 weeks

Add a few shelf-stable meals each shopping trip. Choose a balance of proteins, grains, fruit, and vegetables. Store food in a cool, dry place and confirm your water resupply options.

Plan for 30 days

Make a short list of pantry meals you can repeat. Multiply what each meal uses by how often you’ll serve it. Include cooking water and fuel in your plan for dry foods. Buy what you’ll rotate into regular meals.

Use older supplies first

Put older stock at the front. Record a date to check or use each item, then update the date after you rotate it into regular meals. Discard bulging or leaking cans and cans with deep dents. Replace water you filled yourself every six months.
