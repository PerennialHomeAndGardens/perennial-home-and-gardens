Emergency Preparation

Seven-Day No-Cook Meal Plan

Breakfast, lunch, and dinner from the pantry. Every meal uses foods you can serve cold, with no stove or refrigerator needed before opening.

See the seven-day menu Build a shopping list

Before you open a package

Choose commercially packaged foods marked shelf-stable and ready to eat. Check each label for storage after opening, including tortillas and dressings. Wash your hands and utensils with safe water. When refrigeration is unavailable, open only what you’ll finish at that meal. Discard opened perishable food after two hours at room temperature, or one hour above 90°F. Never taste food to check whether it’s safe.

Also set aside a manual can opener and safe water. For seven days, the CDC baseline is 7 gallons for 1 person, with extra for pets and other household needs. Plan where to refill.

Easy substitutions

  • Fish or chicken Replace a pouch with ¾ cup of ready-to-eat chickpeas or white beans. Update your shopping list for the swaps you choose.
  • Milk Use shelf-stable lactose-free milk or fortified soy milk if it fits your household.
  • Nuts, seeds, or wheat Use a spread or crunchy snack everyone eating it can safely have. Choose labeled gluten-free crackers or tortillas as needed. Check labels for allergens and cross-contact.
  • Lower-sodium choice Pick no-salt-added beans and vegetables and compare labels on pouches and dressing. Drain canned foods and rinse with safe water when available.
