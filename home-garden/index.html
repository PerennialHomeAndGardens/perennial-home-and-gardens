Our Garden Details

Waldorf, Maryland · Zone 7b · Approximately 7.5 acres, with landscaped beds and an orchard along roughly 150 feet of fence.

Our nine fruit trees

Large plum · Molly apple · Liberty apple · Gala apple · Williams Pride apple · Crabapple · Peach · Dwarf peach · Dwarf pear

Berries & edible garden

Five blueberry plants including Pink Lemonade varieties; strawberries, mint, sage, rosemary, cucumbers and other seasonal vegetables.

Flowers, herbs & perennials

Roses (3), peonies (3), lavender (2), salvia, catnip, creeping thyme, comfrey (10–12), blue irises, yarrow, blue globe thistle, blue star sea holly, alliums and wildflowers.

Beds & containers

Raised beds including 12 × 4 ft, 3 × 14 ft and 3 × 6 ft beds, deeper beds, concrete-block borders and large growing containers.
